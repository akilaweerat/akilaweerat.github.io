About Wikunamu.lk

Our Story

Wikunamu.lk was founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to create Sri Lanka's most trusted platform for buying and selling. We understand the importance of local commerce and aim to provide a safe, efficient, and user-friendly platform for all Sri Lankans.

Our Vision

To become Sri Lanka's leading classifieds platform, connecting buyers and sellers across the island while maintaining the highest standards of trust and security.

What We Offer
  • Free ad posting for all users
  • Multiple categories for various needs
  • Secure messaging system
  • User verification system
  • 24/7 customer support
Our Team

Our team consists of dedicated professionals who are passionate about creating the best possible experience for our users. We work tirelessly to ensure that Wikunamu.lk remains a safe and reliable platform for all Sri Lankans.
